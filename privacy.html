Privacy policy: social-studio publisher

Last updated: 2 October 2026

social-studio publisher is a private tool operated by Abdul Muid Hasan Nihal for his own accounts only.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, through the official Google and Meta APIs, to upload and schedule his own videos and to read their basic statistics.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens for those accounts, stored privately on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, store, share or sell data about any other person.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions or in Facebook's Business Integrations settings.
